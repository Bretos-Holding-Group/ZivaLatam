# ZivaID — Product Documentation

This directory contains the controlled conceptual documentation for the **ZivaID** product of **Ziva / ZivaLatam**.

## Authority and governance chain

ZivaID is not an autonomous governance authority. It is a product subordinate to ZivaLatam, which operates under the governance authority of Breto's Holding Group (BHG).

```text
ZivaID
  ↑
ZivaLatam
  ↑
Breto's Holding Group (BHG)
  ↑
BHG Constitution — supreme internal governing authority
```

This is the internal organizational authority chain. Applicable law, regulation and binding external obligations remain superior to internal corporate governance.

No ZivaID product rule, technical implementation, policy or future integration may weaken, bypass, contradict or silently reinterpret a valid higher-level ZivaLatam or BHG directive. Conflicts must be escalated and resolved through explicit governance decisions.
