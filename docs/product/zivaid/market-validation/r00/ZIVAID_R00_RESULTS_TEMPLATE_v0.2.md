## 6. Use-case map

Rank candidate use cases by:

1. concrete problem frequency in the sample;
2. consequence/severity;
3. concentration across independent participants;
4. organizational relevance where applicable;
5. behavioral intent;
6. trust/privacy feasibility;
7. evidence quality.

Do not rank by social engagement alone.

## 7. Trust and objection map

## 9. Negative evidence

- whether the finding suggests narrowing, redesign or stopping.

## 10. Protocol amendments

List every amendment made during R00:

## 11. Decision logic

The final recommendation must distinguish:

- what is demonstrated by the evidence;
- what remains uncertain;
- what is merely plausible;
- what should be tested next.

The report must not claim product-market fit, market size, regulatory approval, production readiness or commercial success from R00 alone.

## 12. Next experiment

Define the smallest next experiment that can reduce the most important remaining uncertainty.

Include:

- hypothesis;
- target participant/organization;
- behavior to observe;
- success/failure condition;
- evidence required;
- maximum scope;
- stop condition.

## 13. Traceability statement

A final R00 decision is considered traceable only when a reviewer can move from:

`Decision → hypothesis → evidence IDs → participant/organization records → research method → protocol version`

without relying on undocumented researcher memory.
