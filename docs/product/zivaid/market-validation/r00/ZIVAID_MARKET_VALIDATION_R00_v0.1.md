# ZivaID Market Validation R00 v0.1

**Status:** Draft — pre-execution  
**Product:** ZivaID  
**Brand / ecosystem:** Ziva / ZivaLatam  
**Holding:** Breto's Holding Group (BHG)  
**Governance chain:** ZivaID → ZivaLatam → BHG → BHG Constitution  
**Baseline:** `ZIVAID_CONCEPTUAL_FOUNDATION_v0.2.md`  

## 1. Objective

Determine, using documented evidence, whether there is a meaningful and sufficiently frequent problem around reusable identity, credentials, verification and selective disclosure that merits further development of ZivaID.

R00 must be capable of concluding:

- continue with the current direction;
- continue with material changes;
- narrow the initial market/use case;
- pause or reject the current hypothesis.

A positive result is not predetermined.

## 2. Primary research question

> Do people and organizations experience enough friction, cost, risk or repetition in identity and credential verification that a reusable, interoperable and service-scoped verification layer would provide meaningful value?

## 3. Secondary questions

1. Which segments experience the problem most strongly?
2. Which use cases have the highest frequency and severity?
3. What alternatives are used today?
4. What prevents users from trusting a reusable identity layer?
5. Which information domains create the strongest demand or strongest objections?
6. Would users accept minimum-disclosure verification?
7. Which organizations could benefit from receiving verified credentials rather than repeatedly collecting evidence?
8. What would cause an organization to pilot such a system?
9. Which initial use case should be prioritized?
10. What evidence would falsify the current ZivaID value proposition?

## 4. Non-objectives

R00 does not attempt to:

- build production ZivaID;
- prove regulatory compliance;
- certify security;
- process real sensitive identity, health, financial or migration records;
- establish legal ownership of data;
- prove product-market fit;
- estimate a final company valuation;
- claim that ZivaID is already a working service.

## 5. Research principles

### 5.1 Problem before solution

Questions must first establish the participant's actual experience before introducing the ZivaID concept.

### 5.2 Falsifiability

Every major hypothesis must have a defined disconfirming signal.

### 5.3 Evidence over enthusiasm

Likes, compliments and generic statements such as "sounds useful" are weak evidence.

Stronger evidence includes a concrete recent problem, repeated occurrence, measurable impact, willingness to change behavior, willingness to participate in a pilot, or organizational willingness to test the workflow.

### 5.4 Negative evidence is first-class evidence

The register must preserve objections, refusals, non-problems and contradictory findings.

### 5.5 No unnecessary personal data

R00 should collect the minimum information necessary for research. Do not request identity documents, RUTs, medical records, financial account information, migration documents, prescriptions or similar sensitive material merely to validate the concept.

### 5.6 No deceptive claims

Researchers must not imply that ZivaID already exists as a production service, has government endorsement, has regulatory approval, or can currently perform the proposed verification functions.

## 6. Research populations

Initial exploratory quotas are targets, not claims of statistical representation:

| Segment | Initial target |
|---|---:|
| Salaried workers | 20 |
| Independent workers | 20 |
| Entrepreneurs / business representatives | 15 |
| Students | 15 |
| Migrants | 20 |
| Other relevant participants | 10 |

Target: approximately 100 short research interactions, followed by 30–50 deeper interviews where feasible.

The quotas may be revised if evidence shows that the problem is concentrated elsewhere. Any material change must be recorded.

## 7. Channels

### Organic digital

Instagram, Facebook and TikTok may be used to distribute neutral research prompts and recruit voluntary participants.

Working research title:

> **¿Cuánto de tu identidad tienes que repetir?**

Internal project name:

> **Campaña ZivaID**

The public-facing research title is intentionally neutral to reduce solution-induced bias.

### Direct field research

Street interviews in Santiago may be conducted using the approved interview protocol. Researchers must not block pedestrian movement, enter restricted areas, or create pressure to participate.

### B2B research

Organizations may be approached to understand verification, onboarding, credential collection, compliance and operational friction.

## 8. Zero-budget constraint

Initial R00 execution is designed to operate with **CLP $0 paid acquisition budget**.

Permitted initial infrastructure may include ordinary phone communication, free forms/spreadsheets, organic social channels and the existing repository.

Paid advertising may be introduced only as a separate experiment after organic evidence has been analyzed, because paid acquisition would change the experiment and introduce a different cost structure.

## 10. Metrics

### Problem metrics

- percentage reporting the target problem;
- frequency of occurrence;
- reported time/cost/friction;
- severity rating;
- number of domains affected.

### Solution metrics

- usefulness rating after the concept is explained;
- strongest perceived use case;
- strongest objection;
- trust requirements;
- willingness to test.

### Behavioral evidence

Higher-value signals include:

- participant volunteers for a follow-up;
- participant introduces another relevant participant;
- participant requests to test the workflow;
- organization offers a process owner for a deeper interview;
- organization expresses willingness to run a pilot.

No single threshold automatically certifies market demand.

## 11. Decision framework

R00 should produce one of four outcomes:

### A — Continue

Evidence supports a meaningful problem and a sufficiently compelling direction.

### B — Narrow / pivot

A problem exists, but the broad ZivaID proposition is too broad or the strongest demand is concentrated in a specific segment/use case.

### C — Rework

Evidence is mixed or the trust/operational barriers are greater than expected.

### D — Stop current hypothesis

The target problem is weak, infrequent, adequately solved by existing alternatives, or the proposed value does not create sufficient user/organizational intent.

The result must include the evidence supporting the decision and the evidence that argues against it.

## 12. Governance and authority

R00 is a subordinate product-validation activity.

```text
ZivaID
   ↑
ZivaLatam
   ↑
BHG
   ↑
BHG Constitution
```

R00 cannot create authority over ZivaLatam or BHG. It cannot amend the ZivaID governance relationship by implication. Any recommendation affecting governance, data ownership, authority delegation, privacy boundaries or corporate structure must be escalated through the appropriate governance process.

Applicable law and binding external obligations remain superior to internal governance.

## 13. Privacy and research ethics

Participants must be told that participation is voluntary and that the research is not a service application.

Do not collect sensitive data unless a future approved protocol explicitly establishes a lawful and necessary basis. For R00, the preferred record is an anonymous participant ID plus research attributes necessary for segmentation.

If a participant spontaneously offers sensitive information, the researcher should avoid recording it unless necessary for the approved research purpose.

## 14. Change control

This v0.1 document is the controlled starting point for R00. Changes to hypotheses, sampling, metrics, privacy rules, governance relationships or decision thresholds must be versioned and traceable.

No field execution should silently alter the protocol.

## 15. Certification boundary

R00 v0.1 is a **research protocol baseline**, not a market-validation result.

Only after evidence collection and analysis may a later R00 result document state a market conclusion.

## 16. Initial success condition

The first success condition is not "ZivaID wins." It is:

> **The research generates enough reliable, traceable and sufficiently diverse evidence to make a rational decision about what ZivaID should do next.**
