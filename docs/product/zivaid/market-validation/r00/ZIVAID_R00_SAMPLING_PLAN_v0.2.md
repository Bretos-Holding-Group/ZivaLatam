# ZivaID R00 — Sampling and Recruitment Plan v0.2

**Status:** Controlled amendment — pre-execution
**Design:** exploratory, non-probability research

## 1. Objective

Produce sufficiently diverse evidence to identify patterns, contradictions and promising wedges without pretending that the sample represents the population of Chile or any other jurisdiction.

## 2. Two-stage sample

### Stage 1 — Short interactions

Target approximately 100 usable short interactions.

Purpose:

- detect recurring problems;
- identify segments and use cases;
- discover contradictions;
- identify candidates for deeper interviews.

### Stage 2 — Deep interviews

Target 30–50 deeper interviews where feasible.

Purpose:

- reconstruct recent incidents;
- quantify frequency and consequence at participant level;
- understand workarounds;
- test the concept only after discovery;
- capture behavioral intent.

These are research targets, not claims of statistical representativeness.

## 3. Segment targets

Initial targets remain:

| Segment | Target |
|---|---:|
| Salaried workers | 20 |
| Independent workers | 20 |
| Entrepreneurs / business representatives | 15 |
| Students | 15 |
| Migrants | 20 |
| Other relevant participants | 10 |

The targets are non-exclusive research quotas. A participant may fit multiple categories, but one primary segment must be assigned consistently for analysis.

## 4. Channel diversity

The initial wave should obtain evidence from at least three channels among:

- Instagram;
- Facebook;
- TikTok;
- direct street interviews;
- voluntary WhatsApp follow-up;
- B2B outreach.

A channel that produces only self-selected supporters must not be treated as equivalent to independent field recruitment.

## 5. Recruitment rules

Recruitment source must be recorded for every participant at a broad level.

Do not recruit only friends, relatives, employees, collaborators or existing supporters of ZivaID. When such participants are included, label the relationship/context because familiarity with the project can create strong bias.

Do not pay participants during the initial zero-budget wave. Do not use prizes or incentives that could materially change participation behavior.

## 6. Duplicate and referral controls

A participant receives one persistent pseudonymous ID for the R00 wave.

If the same person appears through multiple channels, retain one participant ID and record the multiple recruitment paths.

Referral chains must be recorded at a broad level. Multiple participants recruited from the same immediate social group must not automatically be treated as independent corroboration.

## 7. Coverage monitoring

Before interpreting results, report:

- completed interactions;
- usable records;
- quarantined records;
- participants by segment;
- participants by channel;
- participants with prior awareness of ZivaID;
- participants recruited through referrals;
- participants who completed discovery but not concept test;
- B2B organizations/process owners separately.

## 8. Saturation signal

For qualitative discovery, monitor whether the last 10 usable deep interviews continue to produce materially new problem categories, use cases or objections.

If new categories continue appearing, do not claim saturation. Continue until the practical research limit or revise the research scope.

Saturation is a qualitative research signal, not proof that the market has been exhausted.

## 9. Rebalancing rules

Quotas may be rebalanced only when:

- a segment is clearly irrelevant to the observed problem;
- a new segment repeatedly appears as materially relevant;
- a channel creates systematic recruitment bias;
- operational constraints make the original quota impossible.

Before rebalancing, record an amendment explaining the evidence that triggered the change. Do not retrospectively redefine the sample to improve the result.

## 10. Sample limitations

Every R00 result must explicitly state that:

- recruitment is non-random;
- digital participants are self-selected;
- street participants depend on location/time/context;
- quotas are exploratory;
- results cannot be generalized to a population without additional representative research.

## 11. B2B independence

Count organizations, not employees from the same organization, as the primary unit for organization-level corroboration. Distinct process owners may add workflow detail but do not create independent organizational demand evidence unless they represent genuinely separate processes or business units with independent decisions.
