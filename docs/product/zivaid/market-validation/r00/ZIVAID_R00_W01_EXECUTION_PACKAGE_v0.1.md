# ZivaID R00-W01 — Execution Package v0.1

**Status:** Pre-execution controlled package
**Protocol:** R00 v0.2
**Wave:** `R00-W01`
**Instrument:** `R00-INSTR-v0.2`
**Concept:** `CONCEPT-R00-v0.2`
**Paid acquisition:** CLP $0

## 1. Purpose

R00-W01 is the first controlled evidence-collection wave. It exists to test the research protocol in the field while preserving the distinction between problem discovery and concept evaluation.

No market conclusion may be drawn from the wave until the complete evidence package has passed QA and the preregistered analysis has been applied.

## 2. Freeze declaration

Before the first participant is recorded, the operator must freeze and record:

- exact protocol version;
- exact campaign prompt;
- exact discovery questions;
- exact concept-test wording;
- exact field-form version;
- exact analysis-plan version;
- wave ID;
- researcher/operator IDs;
- start date/time.

Any later change requires an amendment ID and prospective application unless explicitly justified and documented.

## 3. First-wave operating target

### Short interactions

Target: **100 usable interactions**.

### Deep interviews

Target: **30–50 usable deep interviews**, subject to operational feasibility.

### Channel minimum

Seek evidence from at least three channels among:

- Instagram;
- Facebook;
- TikTok;
- street interviews;
- voluntary WhatsApp follow-up;
- B2B outreach.

These are diversity targets, not population-representative quotas.

## 4. Daily operating cycle

```text
Recruit
  ↓
Assign P-ID
  ↓
Discovery
  ↓
Record evidence
  ↓
Optional concept test
  ↓
Record behavioral signal
  ↓
QA same day where possible
  ↓
Lock record
  ↓
Update coverage dashboard
```

Do not analyze cumulative results during active collection to decide which questions or participants to pursue. Coverage monitoring is allowed; hypothesis interpretation is deferred until the preregistered analysis stage.

## 5. Participant identity

Use pseudonymous IDs only:

`P-001`, `P-002`, etc.

Organizations:

`ORG-R00-001`, `ORG-R00-002`, etc.

Follow-up contact information, when voluntarily provided, must be stored separately from evidence records.

## 6. Recruitment log

For every participant record:

- P-ID;
- date;
- channel;
- broad recruitment source;
- prior awareness of ZivaID: yes/no/unknown;
- referral relationship: none/general referral/immediate social group;
- primary segment;
- interview status;
- evidence status.

## 7. Field rules

### Before discovery

Do not describe ZivaID or claim that repeated verification is a known problem.

### During discovery

Use neutral probes. Prefer concrete recent incidents over opinions.

### Before concept exposure

Complete the discovery section and record whether a relevant problem was actually found.

### During concept exposure

Read/use the frozen `CONCEPT-R00-v0.2` wording. Do not improvise persuasive claims.

### After concept exposure

Capture both positive and negative reactions, including trust barriers and reasons not to use it.

## 8. Mandatory evidence separation

Every participant record must preserve separate fields for:

- problem evidence;
- severity/consequence;
- current workaround;
- contradiction;
- concept reaction;
- behavioral signal.

Never replace these with one overall score.

## 9. Deviation protocol

A deviation occurs when the operator:

- changes a question materially;
- skips a required discovery step;
- exposes the concept early;
- uses a non-frozen concept description;
- records an unclear participant identity;
- stores prohibited sensitive data;
- changes participant selection because of an emerging hypothesis;
- loses instrument/version provenance.

Assign a deviation ID such as `DEV-R00-W01-001`.

For each deviation record:

- date;
- operator;
- affected participant/evidence IDs;
- exact deviation;
- reason;
- whether the record remains usable;
- remediation;
- prospective impact.

## 10. Daily QA gate

A day's records are ready for lock only when:

- [ ] P-IDs are unique.
- [ ] Channel is recorded.
- [ ] Recruitment source is recorded.
- [ ] Discovery/concept sequence is known.
- [ ] Instrument and concept IDs are present where applicable.
- [ ] Contradictions are preserved.
- [ ] Unknown values remain unknown.
- [ ] No prohibited sensitive source material is stored.
- [ ] Deviations are logged.
- [ ] Quarantined records are not counted as usable.

## 11. Stop / pause triggers

Pause collection immediately if:

1. participants are being systematically led toward the hypothesis;
2. operators begin improvising materially different questions;
3. the campaign is collecting sensitive identity documents or credentials;
4. duplicate participants cannot be reliably controlled;
5. a channel creates an obvious recruitment distortion;
6. the instrument version cannot be established;
7. evidence integrity cannot be maintained.

The operator may resume only after the cause is documented and the relevant amendment/control is established.

## 12. Field safety and privacy

Do not photograph identity documents. Do not collect RUT, passport number, migration case number, financial account information, passwords, authentication codes, medical information or exact home addresses.

Do not pressure anyone to participate. Participation is voluntary and may stop at any time.

For street research, do not obstruct pedestrians, enter restricted premises, or represent the research as official government, financial, immigration or identity verification.

## 13. Wave closure

R00-W01 closes when one of the following is reached:

- planned collection target is reached;
- operational limit is reached and documented;
- a stop condition requires termination;
- evidence indicates the research scope must be amended materially.

Closure requires:

- locked evidence register;
- recruitment summary;
- deviation register;
- quarantine/exclusion summary;
- sampling limitations;
- protocol version record;
- analysis-plan application;
- results report;
- next-experiment recommendation.

## 14. Certification boundary

R00-W01 does not certify that ZivaID has product-market fit, market size, regulatory approval, legal compliance, security readiness or commercial viability.

It certifies only the quality and traceability of the evidence generated under the controlled research protocol.
