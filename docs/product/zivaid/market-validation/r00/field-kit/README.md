# ZivaID Market Validation R00 — Field Kit v0.1

**Status:** Operational draft

This directory contains the controlled field materials for executing the already-approved ZivaID Market Validation R00 protocol.

## Authority

ZivaID → ZivaLatam → BHG → BHG Constitution.

The field kit may operationalize the approved R00 protocol but may not change its hypotheses, evidence rules, privacy rules, authority chain, or decision criteria.

## Scope

The field kit supports:

- short street interviews in Santiago;
- digital recruitment through Instagram, Facebook and TikTok;
- structured note-taking;
- anonymous participant coding;
- follow-up consent separated from evidence records;
- pilot-readiness tracking.

## Core rule

**Investigate the problem before presenting ZivaID.**

No participant should be required to provide identity documents or sensitive personal information.
