## Rules

- Never record RUT, passport number, document images, medical records, financial account details, passwords or immigration case numbers.
- Do not infer sensitive attributes.
- Use `unknown` rather than guessing.
- Separate what the participant said from the interviewer's interpretation.
- One participant = one participant ID, even if they provide multiple statements.
