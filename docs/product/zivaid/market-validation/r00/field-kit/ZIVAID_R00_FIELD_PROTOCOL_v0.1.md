# ZivaID R00 Field Protocol v0.1

## 1. Purpose

Provide a repeatable, low-cost procedure for collecting first-hand market evidence in Santiago and through organic digital channels.

## 2. Required sequence

### Phase A — Before presenting ZivaID

Ask about actual experiences with identity, documentation, verification and access to services. Do not mention ZivaID until the participant's experience has been captured.

### Phase B — Concept reaction

Present the neutral concept:

> Imagine a system that lets a person prove only the specific information a service needs, without giving that service access to unrelated information.

Ask for reaction, concerns and useful contexts.

### Phase C — Behavioural intent

Ask what service they would use it for first, what would stop them, and whether they would voluntarily participate in a future pilot.

## 3. Street recruitment

Opening:

> Hola. Estoy haciendo una investigación sobre cómo las personas utilizan sus documentos e información personal para hacer trámites y acceder a servicios. No estoy vendiendo nada. ¿Te puedo hacer unas preguntas? Toma unos cinco minutos.

Rules:

- participation is voluntary;
- do not block, follow or pressure people;
- do not approach minors for the controlled R00 sample;
- do not claim to represent a government, bank, hospital or other institution;
- do not request documents or sensitive personal information;
- do not photograph or record participants without a separate appropriate consent process;
- stop immediately if the person declines or asks to stop;
- prioritize personal safety and use public, ordinary settings.

## 4. Sampling

Use exploratory quotas only. They are not statistically representative claims. Seek diversity across relevant contexts without requiring disclosure of sensitive attributes.

Avoid recruiting an entire sample from one location, friend group or online community.

## 5. Evidence handling

Use participant codes such as `P-R00-001`. Keep optional follow-up contact information separate from evidence records.

Do not place names, RUTs, passport numbers, medical information, financial account details, immigration case details, passwords or copies of documents in the R00 evidence register.

## 6. Interviewer discipline

Do not paraphrase a participant into a stronger claim than they made. Distinguish direct experience, observation, interpretation and hypothetical opinion. Negative evidence is first-class evidence.

## 7. Digital channels

Use Instagram, Facebook and TikTok to recruit voluntarily. The public message must describe research rather than claim that a finished product exists.

Preferred framing: **¿Cuánto de tu identidad tienes que repetir?**

## 8. Operational pilot

Initial field test:

- 10 street interviews;
- 10 digital responses.

This is an instrument test, not a claim of market validation. After the pilot, record execution issues separately from market findings. Do not retroactively change evidence already collected.

## 9. Authority and release boundary

This protocol is subordinate to the approved R00 documentation and governance chain:

**ZivaID → ZivaLatam → BHG → BHG Constitution.**

`BLACKBOX_PASS` verifies the evidence-admission engine but does not itself authorize real-data collection. Changes to controlled rules require a versioned documentation change before prospective use.
