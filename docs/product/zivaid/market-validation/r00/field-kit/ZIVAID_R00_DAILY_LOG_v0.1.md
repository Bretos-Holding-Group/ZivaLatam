## Daily discipline

1. Complete the log before beginning the next field session.
2. Do not rewrite participant statements to make them stronger.
3. Do not convert operational problems into market findings.
4. If the protocol appears defective, pause and escalate rather than silently changing it.
5. Any prospective protocol change must be versioned before use.
