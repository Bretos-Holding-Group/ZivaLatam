# BHG Future Integration Alignment

## Purpose

This record documents ZivaLatam's independent alignment with the architectural and governance principles intended to facilitate a possible future integration with BHG.

It does not establish present BHG ownership, control, subsidiary status, legal subordination, or governance authority.

## Current State

ZivaLatam is an independent operational entity/product with its own identity, systems, and operational rules.

Current relationship:

**INDEPENDENT / FUTURE-INTEGRATION-CANDIDATE**

ZivaLatam may adopt compatible standards and architectural principles in advance so that a future integration, if approved, does not require fundamental reconstruction of its systems.

## Alignment Principle

Alignment with BHG does not equal affiliation with BHG.

Shared standards, architecture, documentation methods, tooling, or strategic intent do not independently create ownership, control, or subsidiary status.

ZivaLatam retains authority over its current operational matters except where a separate valid agreement or legal instrument provides otherwise.

## Future Integration

ZivaLatam acknowledges the strategic possibility of becoming the first BHG subsidiary in the future, subject to a separate formal integration process and the applicable legal, corporate, governance, financial, technical, and operational requirements.

The future integrated state is:

**INTEGRATED**

This state must not be treated as effective until the required formal approvals and instruments are completed.

## Bilateral Documentation Principle

BHG maintains its own record of future integration intent from the BHG institutional perspective.

This document records ZivaLatam's independent perspective. Neither document creates the future relationship by itself.

## Integration Gate

Before integration is recognized as effective, the applicable process must establish:

- current ownership and legal status;
- formal integration instrument(s);
- governance authority and boundaries;
- organizational and subsidiary relationship;
- technical and documentary dependencies;
- financial and operational implications;
- registry updates;
- post-integration verification.

## Non-Authority Clause

This document does not:

- establish BHG ownership or control;
- establish a subsidiary relationship;
- surrender current ZivaLatam operational authority;
- create legal obligations that do not otherwise exist;
- replace a future legal or corporate integration instrument;
- authorize BHG governance over ZivaLatam today.

It records ZivaLatam's current independent status and its deliberate compatibility with a possible future BHG integration.
