# MVP Freeze Policy

## Status

Active

---

## Purpose

Protect the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) from uncontrolled scope changes.

This policy ensures that development remains focused on delivering the approved MVP before introducing additional features, improvements or architectural expansions.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Human contributors
- AI assistants
- Product decisions
- Engineering decisions
- Feature requests
- Architecture proposals

During the entire MVP development phase.

---

## Policy Statement

> The approved MVP scope shall remain stable until the MVP has been completed or formally revised.

---

## Rules

### Rule 1 — No Unapproved Features

No new features may be implemented unless they belong to the approved MVP scope.

---

### Rule 2 — Ideas Are Never Lost

Ideas generated during development must not interrupt the current sprint.

Instead, they shall be captured and classified for future evaluation.

---

### Rule 3 — Scope Changes Require Approval

Any modification to the MVP scope requires explicit approval from the CEO and must be documented before implementation.

---

### Rule 4 — Prioritize Completion

Completing the planned MVP always takes priority over expanding it.

---

### Rule 5 — Controlled Innovation

Innovation is encouraged.

Implementation is controlled.

Every new idea must follow the established engineering process before becoming part of the product.

---

### Rule 6 — Future-Oriented Thinking

Ideas that do not belong to the MVP should be preserved for future phases instead of being discarded.

---

## Exceptions

Critical security vulnerabilities.

Critical legal or regulatory requirements.

Critical defects that prevent the MVP from functioning.

All exceptions must be documented.

---

## Compliance

Compliance is verified during:

- Product Review
- Sprint Planning
- Engineering Review
- Architecture Audit

Any implementation outside the approved MVP scope is considered non-compliant.
