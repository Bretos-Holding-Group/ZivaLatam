# Engineering Policies

## Purpose

This directory contains the permanent operational policies of the Ziva Engineering System (ZES).

Policies define how Ziva Latam is built, documented, reviewed, maintained and evolved.

Unlike Architecture Decision Records (ADRs), which document architectural decisions made at a specific point in time, policies define the permanent operational rules that must be followed throughout the project's lifecycle.

These policies are mandatory for:

- Human contributors
- AI assistants
- External collaborators
- Future engineering teams

---

## Engineering Principles

Every policy must:

- Support the Engineering Charter.
- Never contradict an accepted ADR.
- Be clear, enforceable and measurable.
- Have a defined purpose.
- Be versioned when modified.

---

## Initial Policy Set

The initial governance layer includes:

1. Documentation First Policy
2. Repository Materialization Policy
3. MVP Freeze Policy
4. AI Collaboration Policy
5. Decision Escalation Policy

Additional policies may be incorporated as the Ziva Engineering System evolves.

---

## Relationship with ADRs

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs):

- Explain **why** a technical decision was made.

Engineering Policies:

- Explain **how** the project operates every day.

Both are complementary and together define the governance model of the Ziva Engineering System.
