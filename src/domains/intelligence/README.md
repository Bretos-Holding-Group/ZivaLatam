# Intelligence Domain

**Domain:** Ziva Intelligence System

**Status:** Reserved

**Owner:** Ziva Engineering System (ZES)

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Intelligence Domain is responsible for transforming structured outputs
from Trust and Credit domains into analytical, predictive and decision-support
signals.

It represents the cognitive layer of the Ziva ecosystem.

---

# 2. Core Responsibility

The Intelligence Domain MUST:

- analyze structured trust data
- interpret credit and financial signals
- generate deterministic insights
- support decision-making processes
- maintain full auditability of reasoning paths

---

# 3. What This Domain Is NOT

The Intelligence Domain MUST NOT:

- perform autonomous decision execution
- replace Trust or Credit logic
- operate on raw unvalidated data
- introduce non-deterministic reasoning
- access external systems directly

---

# 4. Inputs

The Intelligence Domain operates on:

- Trust Domain outputs
- Credit Domain decisions
- structured and validated datasets

No raw evidence is allowed.

---

# 5. Outputs

The Intelligence Domain produces:

- analytical insights
- structured prediction signals
- decision support artifacts
- behavioral pattern interpretations

All outputs must be deterministic, traceable and reproducible.

---

# 6. Core Principle

Intelligence in Ziva is not artificial autonomy.

It is:

> structured interpretation of deterministic financial and behavioral data.

---

# 7. Internal Structure (Planned)

The domain will eventually include:

- analyzers/
- predictors/
- models/
- rules/
- aggregators/
- explainers/
- types/
- tests/

No implementation exists at this stage.

---

# 8. Dependency Rules

The Intelligence Domain MAY depend on:

- Trust Domain (mandatory)
- Credit Domain (mandatory)
- Kernel execution layer
- shared contract definitions

The Intelligence Domain MUST NOT depend on:

- Evidence Domain directly
- external APIs
- unstructured inputs
- identity systems

---

# 9. Governance Principles

This domain follows:

- Documentation First
- Deterministic Systems
- Auditability by Default
- Privacy by Design
- Environmental Efficiency by Design
- Regulatory Safety Boundary Layer (ZES-RSBL)

---

# 10. System Role

Intelligence acts as a **decision-support layer**, not a decision executor.

It informs, but does not act.

It interprets, but does not override upstream domains.

---

# 11. Relationship to Other Domains

Evidence → Trust → Credit → Intelligence

- Evidence defines reality signals
- Trust evaluates integrity
- Credit determines financial capacity
- Intelligence interprets system behavior

---

# 12. Current Status

The Intelligence Domain is defined but not implemented.

It completes the cognitive layer of the initial Ziva architecture stack.
